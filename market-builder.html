Market Rate Builder

Turn a spreadsheet export into a rate file the toolkit can read
Author wherever you like. Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers, LibreOffice, a text editor. Export CSV, paste it below, and this validates it and writes the JSON. The spreadsheet stays a good place to type forty rows of numbers; it just stops being something the toolkit depends on.

About this dataset

The factors are the house position, in one place. Published low × low factor and published high × high factor set the band; mid position places the recommendation inside it. Making a whole dataset more or less conservative is three numbers here, not a re-export of every row.

Tiers

id,label,multiplier,rooftopPremium — one per line. The multiplier scales equipment and space adders; the rooftop premium is what an elevated structure carries over ground in the same market.

Metros

metro,tier,publishedLow,publishedHigh,basis — basis is published, analogy or benchmark. Say which: a figure extrapolated from a neighbouring market is not the same kind of thing as a published one, and six months later nobody remembers which was which.

Equipment adders and space rates

Adders: category,item,baseline,tierScaled,note. Space: spaceType,unit,baseline,tierScaled,note. One baseline per item. Per-tier figures are the baseline times the tier multiplier and are computed at load — set tierScaled to false for the rows where that does not hold, rather than writing the numbers out.
