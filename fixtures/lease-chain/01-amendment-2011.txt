              FIRST AMENDMENT TO COMMUNICATIONS SITE LEASE AGREEMENT

THIS FIRST AMENDMENT is dated September 15, 2011, by and between PLACEHOLDER
HOLDINGS LLC ("Lessor") and SAMPLE WIRELESS INC. ("Lessee").

WHEREAS, the parties entered into a Communications Site Lease Agreement dated
May 1, 2006 (the "Agreement"); and

WHEREAS, the parties wish to amend the Agreement as set forth below;

NOW THEREFORE the parties agree as follows:

1.  LEASE FEE.

    Effective October 1, 2011, Section 3(a) of the Agreement is deleted in its
    entirety and replaced with the following:

        "Lessee shall pay to Lessor a lease fee of ONE THOUSAND SIX HUNDRED
        FIFTY AND NO/100 DOLLARS ($1,650.00) per month (the 'Lease Fee'),
        payable in advance on the first day of each calendar month."

    For the avoidance of doubt, Section 3(b) of the Agreement remains in full
    force and effect, and the Lease Fee shall continue to increase by three
    percent (3%) on each anniversary of the Commencement Date.

2.  PREMISES.

    The Premises is expanded by an additional four hundred (400) square feet of
    ground space adjacent to the existing compound, as depicted on Exhibit A-1
    attached hereto. No additional lease fee is payable in respect of the
    expanded area.

3.  ACCESS.

    Section 5 of the Agreement is amended to provide that Lessee shall have
    access to the Premises twenty-four (24) hours per day, seven (7) days per
    week, without prior notice, provided that Lessee shall use reasonable
    efforts to notify Lessor of any access outside normal business hours.

4.  OTHER TERMS.

    Except as amended herein, the Agreement remains in full force and effect.
