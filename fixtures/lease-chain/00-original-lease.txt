                    COMMUNICATIONS SITE LEASE AGREEMENT

THIS COMMUNICATIONS SITE LEASE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made as of
May 1, 2006 (the "Commencement Date"), by and between PLACEHOLDER HOLDINGS LLC,
a limited liability company ("Lessor"), and SAMPLE WIRELESS INC., a corporation
("Lessee").

                                  RECITALS

WHEREAS, Lessor is the owner of certain real property located at 1 Example Road,
Springfield, ZZ 00000 (the "Land"); and

WHEREAS, Lessee desires to lease a portion of the Land for the purpose of
constructing and operating a communications facility;

NOW THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1.  PREMISES.

    Lessor leases to Lessee approximately two thousand five hundred (2,500)
    square feet of ground space, together with the tower and improvements
    constructed thereon, as depicted on Exhibit A attached hereto (the
    "Premises"). Lessee shall also have a non-exclusive easement for ingress
    and egress over the Land.

2.  TERM.

    (a) The initial term of this Agreement shall be five (5) years commencing on
        the Commencement Date and expiring on April 30, 2011 (the "Initial
        Term").

    (b) Lessee shall have the option to extend this Agreement for four (4)
        additional terms of five (5) years each (each, an "Extension Term"),
        upon the same terms and conditions, provided that Lessee gives Lessor
        written notice of its election to extend not less than one hundred and
        eighty (180) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

    (c) The Initial Term together with any exercised Extension Terms are
        referred to as the "Term".

3.  LEASE FEE.

    (a) Lessee shall pay to Lessor a lease fee of ONE THOUSAND TWO HUNDRED AND
        NO/100 DOLLARS ($1,200.00) per month (the "Lease Fee"), payable in
        advance on the first day of each calendar month.

    (b) Commencing on the first anniversary of the Commencement Date and on each
        anniversary thereafter, the Lease Fee shall increase by three percent
        (3%) over the Lease Fee payable during the immediately preceding twelve
        (12) month period.

    (c) The Lease Fee is inclusive of all charges for the Premises and Lessee
        shall not be separately charged for the area occupied.

4.  USE.

    Lessee may use the Premises for the transmission and reception of wireless
    communications signals and for the construction, maintenance and operation
    of a communications facility. Any material alteration to the configuration
    of Lessee's equipment shall require the prior written consent of Lessor,
    such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

5.  ACCESS.

    Lessee shall have access to the Premises during normal business hours,
    Monday through Friday, upon twenty-four (24) hours prior notice to Lessor.
    In the event of an emergency affecting the operation of the communications
    facility, Lessee shall have access at any time, provided Lessee notifies
    Lessor as soon as practicable thereafter.

6.  UTILITIES.

    Lessee shall be responsible for all utilities consumed at the Premises and
    shall, where practicable, install a separate meter at its own cost.

7.  ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING.

    Lessee shall not assign this Agreement or sublet the Premises without the
    prior written consent of Lessor. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Lessee may
    assign this Agreement to any parent, subsidiary or affiliate of Lessee, or
    to any entity acquiring substantially all of Lessee's assets in the market
    in which the Premises is located, without the consent of Lessor.

8.  CO-LOCATION.

    Lessee may permit one or more additional communications providers to locate
    equipment on the tower, and no additional fee shall be payable to Lessor in
    respect of any such co-location.

9.  TAXES.

    Lessor shall pay all real property taxes assessed against the Land. Lessee
    shall pay any personal property taxes assessed against its equipment.

10. INSURANCE.

    Lessee shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of
    not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000) per occurrence and shall name
    Lessor as an additional insured.

11. DEFAULT.

    In the event Lessee fails to pay the Lease Fee when due and such failure
    continues for thirty (30) days after written notice from Lessor, or fails to
    perform any other obligation under this Agreement and such failure continues
    for thirty (30) days after written notice, Lessor may terminate this
    Agreement.

12. TERMINATION.

    Lessee may terminate this Agreement upon written notice to Lessor if Lessee
    is unable to obtain or maintain any governmental approval necessary for the
    operation of the communications facility. Upon any such termination Lessee
    shall pay to Lessor a termination fee equal to six (6) months of the
    then-current Lease Fee.

13. SURRENDER.

    Upon expiration or termination, Lessee shall remove its equipment and
    restore the Premises to substantially its original condition, reasonable
    wear and tear excepted.

14. NOTICES.

    All notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth
    below or such other address as a party may designate.

15. QUIET ENJOYMENT.

    Lessor covenants that Lessee shall peaceably hold and enjoy the Premises
    during the Term.

16. GOVERNING LAW.

    This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state in which the Land
    is located.
