Audit-stage screening — is this lease out of line, and what should we ask for?
admin
This is a screening tool, not an appraisal. The bands are assembled from published market commentary and industry rules of thumb, and published ranges in this market disagree by roughly 2×. Use it to decide which leases deserve a closer look and where to open — not to reprice a site on its own. It writes marketBenchmark and equipment into the deal file; the comparator prices the scenario you build from it.

Band derivation admin

Where the recommended band comes from. Published ranges in this market disagree by roughly 2×, and not randomly: landlord-side consultancies that sell representation publish the high ranges, while transaction-weighted sources publish materially lower ones. The workbook therefore discounts the published band before recommending anything.
